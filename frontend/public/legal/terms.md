## 1. What Sutra is

Sutra is a privacy-first, local-first ambient-memory app. You capture a conversation; Sutra transcribes it, separates who said what, and distills it into durable memory (summary, facts, decisions, action items, events, and a personal knowledge graph) that you can search and ask questions of. Your memory is stored **encrypted on your own device**, and your **raw audio is never stored** — it is processed in the moment and discarded (see the Privacy Policy).

Sutra is a **phone app (Android), a desktop app, and a web/PWA build** — it is not a hardware wearable. It is a **single-user** product: it has no teams, sharing, or multi-user features.

The Service uses **cloud-assisted AI processing** provided by third parties to transcribe and analyze your conversations. AI output can be imperfect (see Section 10).
